Mohar

Verifiable records of teacher eligibility checks

A teacher holds a signed eligibility credential from the body that issued their certificate. When a school checks it, Mohar records that the check happened: which school, when, for what purpose and under what consent. The teacher discloses only the eligibility claims; date of birth, category, family names and marks stay hidden. Each record is anchored to Ethereum Sepolia and to Bitcoin, and anyone holding its export can confirm it later without the school or the platform.

  1. Teacher presents the consented claims to the school.
  2. School verifies them and signs a receipt.
  3. Log anchors the receipt's salted commitment to Sepolia and Bitcoin.

SIMULATED. The issuer, credentials and certificates behind the sample bundle are simulated test data for invented people.

Check a receipt

Paste an export bundle or use the sample. The page checks the school's signature, the log entry, the Merkle path, the Sepolia anchor and the Bitcoin timestamp, and sends nothing to the school or the platform.
